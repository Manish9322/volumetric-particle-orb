# Volumetric Particle Orb

**Volumetric Particle Orb** is an interactive 3D three-layer particle sphere rendered in real time with custom GLSL shaders on a clean monochrome canvas. Grab and rotate the entire sphere freely in 3D space with smooth momentum inertia, glide across the surface for subtle pointer repulsion and tilt, click or tap anywhere on the orb to trigger propagating geodesic fluid waves across all three concentric layers, and open the collapsible bottom dock (`H`) to tune layer geometry, unified particle sizing, rotation speed, wave physics, and black-and-white tonal palettes.

The platform is built with a modern technology stack, including **Next.js**, **React**, **TypeScript**, **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber**, **Motion**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Lucide React**, ensuring a highly performant, responsive, and engaging user experience.
